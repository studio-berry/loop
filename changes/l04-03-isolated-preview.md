Category: changed
Audience: operators
Breaking-Change: no
Summary: Technical and visual previews are now explicit, plan-bound, nonpublishing computations. Each preview carries a `fidelity_mode`: the technical preview is `exact` (a structural comparison over the serialized and reopened candidate bytes) and the visual preview is `simulated` (a rendered simulation, never proof of print safety). This describes artifact-computation fidelity, not the interactive canvas render authority of `PreviewStateModel::Authority` (none/approximate/simulated/authoritative); the two vocabularies overlap only on `simulated`. Both builders now fail closed on the supplied plan digest: an empty, malformed, or mismatched digest is refused before any candidate write, recomputed as `computeOperationPlanDigest(transaction.plans(), transaction.sourceSha256(), transaction.savePolicy())`. The transaction's cancellation control now reaches both builders and the serialization path, so a cancelled preview reports `incomplete` with reason `cancelled`. Under the new residue contract a cancelled or failed preview removes what that call created — its candidate file, its rendered PNGs, and a `mkpath`-created parent directory when now empty — so cancelling or failing a preview leaves no externally visible artifact or approval. Reviewer can check `previewLeavesSourceBytesUntouched`, `cancelledPreviewLeavesNoArtifacts`, `previewRefusesPlanDigestMismatch`, `previewCrashLeavesNoPartialArtifactAndNoApproval`, and the Previews section in docs/GOVERNED_EXECUTION.md.
