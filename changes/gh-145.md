Summary: Close out gh-145. Fix the screen-to-page tolerance conversion, which divided a pixel slack by the zoom alone: the zoom is only half the page-to-screen scale, so on a 96 dpi display a 2 px hit tolerance reached closer to 8 px. HitTestDispatcher and DragSnapper now take screen pixels per page unit, ViewportController exposes it as pageUnitToPixel(), and InteractionController pushes the live value before every hit test. Fix the gh-145 AC7 counters, which ran candidate counts through the trace's duration formatter and reported twelve candidates as a p50 of 0.000012 ms; counts now use countPercentileObject and unsuffixed p50/p95/p99 keys. Wire the snapping seam into the product: DragSnapper and PageBoxSnapProvider existed with no caller at all, so EditorHost::bindCanvas now builds a provider over the same PageBoxHitTestSource the dispatcher hit-tests and unbindCanvas drops it. Add the test half of gh-145: UnitTestsDragSnapper covers the snapper's ranking, tie-break, zoom-converted threshold and the disabled/empty/zero-threshold no-ops; UnitTestsInteractionController gains edge-of-viewport dragging with pointer capture, snapping below and above the hysteresis threshold, preview-not-document separation, the Alt suppress modifier sampled per move rather than latched at press, tolerance following the live view scale, the AC7 counters, and the AC6 case where index churn alone does not invalidate a drag but a revision change refuses the commit; UnitTestsViewportController pins the zoom anchor to a declared half-pixel tolerance across a zoom sweep and on 90/180/270 rotated pages; UnitTestsHitTestSource covers the tolerance-expanded query crossing a cell boundary and de-duplicating items that span several cells. Document the corrected conversion and the product wiring in docs/INTERACTION_CONTRACT.md.
