Summary: Read-only annotation review and document signature status for the Quick shell, with a signature-invalidation disclosure and publish gate. Annotations from the open revision are listed in a review column (page, kind, author, contents, subject) and navigable to their page region; they are painted as a read-only overlay band with their own bound so a heavily annotated document cannot crowd findings, selection, or steering primitives, and they are never interaction targets. Signature status is verified off the UI thread as a governed job whose outcome crosses back only through the scheduler, and the DocumentFacade reduces verification results to five distinct operator-facing states (not-signed, valid, invalid, unknown, modified-after-signing) with tokens fenced by request, generation, and revision; only a verified result reads as a pass, and "no signature present" is never folded into another state. The correction-plan preview and the approval disclose when a plan's merged save policy invalidates signatures, and the Action List execution refuses to publish an invalidating plan over a signed document until the exact plan digest is acknowledged; a replan or revision change cannot inherit the acknowledgement.
